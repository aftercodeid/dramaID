## Account & Privacy

<div class="faq-list">
<details class="faq-item">
<summary>How do I sign in to DramaID?</summary>
<p>With your Google or Apple account. We receive your email address and display name from that provider, and never receive or store your Google/Apple password. More in our <a href="/en/privacy">Privacy Policy</a>.</p>
</details>
<details class="faq-item">
<summary>Forgot how to sign in?</summary>
<p>Try signing in again with the same method (Google or Apple) you used when you first registered.</p>
</details>
<details class="faq-item">
<summary>What data does DramaID collect?</summary>
<p>Account data (email, display name), watch activity (progress, history, watchlist), comment content, and standard technical data (IP address, device type, app version). We don't use third-party ad SDKs and we don't sell your personal data. Full detail in our <a href="/en/privacy">Privacy Policy</a>.</p>
</details>
<details class="faq-item">
<summary>How do I delete my account?</summary>
<p>Via <strong>Settings → Profile → Delete Account</strong> in the app, or through the <a href="/en/delete-account">Delete Account</a> page if you can't access the app.</p>
</details>
<details class="faq-item">
<summary>What's the minimum age to create an account?</summary>
<p>13 years old, per our <a href="/en/terms">Terms of Service</a>.</p>
</details>
</div>

## Content & Copyright

<div class="faq-list">
<details class="faq-item">
<summary>Does DramaID store video files?</summary>
<p>No. DramaID does not host or store video files — the app displays metadata from public sources and links to third-party providers for watching.</p>
</details>
<details class="faq-item">
<summary>Where do titles, synopses, and posters come from?</summary>
<p>From public sources such as MyDramaList. This metadata is not your personal data and isn't tied to your account.</p>
</details>
<details class="faq-item">
<summary>I'm a rights holder and found infringing content. What do I do?</summary>
<p>Send us a complaint following the process described in our <a href="/en/dmca">DMCA Policy</a>.</p>
</details>
</div>
